STYLE: Photorealistic live-action documentary footage, shot on a real camera in a real small-town launderette at night. Handheld, natural, unhurried camera, shallow depth of field, slight film grain. Real flat fluorescent strip light, a little green, real reflections on the steel machines and the wet floor. The stylized fox character from [Image1] and [Image2] is placed into this real world the way a computer-animated bear is placed into a live-action family film: his face and design stay exactly as the references, with realistic dense orange fur, individual strands catching the strip light, real contact shadows under his boots. Everything around him is real and ordinary.

PLACE: A small, slightly worn launderette at night. A row of white front-loading washing machines, a wall of stacked steel tumble dryers with round glass doors, plastic bucket chairs bolted to a rail, a long folding table, a mop bucket in the corner, a big front window with rain running down it and a wet street outside with car lights passing. No readable signs.

CHARACTER: One fox, compact small frame, slim build, fluffy orange fur, cream cheeks, heavy-lidded golden yellow eyes, dark blue rolled jeans, brown boots, thick orange tail with a cream tip. In this film he wears a plain white t-shirt, because his dark charcoal hoodie from the references is in the dryer. Grumpy and unimpressed: eyes alert under flat lids, brows a little low, completely bored expression. Never sleepy, never angry, never shocked. Keep his exact face, fur colour and size the same in every shot. He has exactly two arms and two paws. He moves slowly and calmly the whole time.

PEOPLE: One older woman in a raincoat folding towels at the far end of the folding table. She stays at the table. She only glances at him once.

0-3s: Wide shot from OUTSIDE on the wet street at night, looking in through the big rainy front window. The whole launderette under strip lights. The fox sits alone on a plastic chair in his white t-shirt facing the dryers, paws on his knees. The woman folds towels at the far table.

3-6s: Inside. Close on the round glass dryer door: dark charcoal clothes tumbling, slowing, stopping. The fox steps into the frame beside the dryer.

6-10s: Medium shot, facing him, open dryer behind him. He pulls out his dark charcoal hoodie. It has shrunk to the size of a hoodie for a small toy, no bigger than his own head. He holds it up in front of his chest by its two tiny shoulders, one in each paw. It covers only a small patch of his white t-shirt. He looks at it with a long, flat, bored stare. He does not move.

10-12s: His point of view: his two orange paws in the foreground holding the tiny hoodie up. Beyond it, the woman at the folding table has stopped folding and stares at him.

12-15s: Medium shot facing him. He squeezes the tiny hoodie on over his head. It is far too small: it stops high on his chest like a crop top, his white t-shirt and fluffy belly fur fully showing beneath it, the sleeves end halfway up his upper arms, the tiny hood sits perched on top of his head between his two tall ears like a small cap, his ears stay out and visible. He tugs the hem down once with both paws. It springs straight back up to his chest.

15-17s: Hold on him, standing still in the tiny hoodie, arms at his sides, staring flat and bored straight into the camera. Total dignity. Nothing moves.

17-20s: He turns and walks slowly out of the open front door into the rain. Seen from behind: the tiny hoodie riding high on his back, his white t-shirt below it, tail swinging. A car passes in the rain outside. He does not hurry.

SOUND: Dryers humming, a dryer slowing to a stop and a click, the dryer door opening, fabric, rain on the window, the door bell, footsteps on wet pavement, a car passing on a wet road. No music, no dialogue, no narration.

NEGATIVE: No cartoon or animated set, no painted background, no game look, everything except the fox is real. No readable words, signs or logos anywhere. No second fox, no change to his face, no feral animal on all fours, no human hands on the fox, no extra arms or paws, no distorted anatomy, no floating. The hoodie stays tiny the whole time and never grows back to full size; it never reaches his waist and the hood never covers his ears or face. The woman never comes over or speaks.
